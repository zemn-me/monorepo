# Temple Church, London — wireframe references

This is a schematic architectural illustration, not a measured reconstruction.
Coordinates are metres, Y-up, with the chancel extending east along +X.

## Published dimensions

George Godwin and John Britton, *The Churches of London* (1839), Temple Church,
p. 31, gives measurements **within the walls**: circular area diameter 58 ft
(17.6784 m); choir length 82 ft (24.9936 m), width 58 ft (17.6784 m), and height
37 ft (11.2776 m). These are internal dimensions, not an exterior survey.

## Plans and architectural references

- [The Inner Temple: History of the West Doorway](https://publications.innertemple.org.uk/yearbook/articles/2025-2026/history-of-the-west-doorway-temple-church/):
  Figure 4a reproduces W. H. Godfrey and M. B. Honeybourne's plan; Figures 2a and
  4c show the Round and chancel in December 2024. The article gives approximate
  west doorway dimensions of 4 m high by 4.4 m wide. The model follows the round
  nave, six-column inner arcade, rectangular four-bay chancel, triple lancets,
  buttresses, and west porch. Reference images are not redistributed.
- [1854 Pictorial Handbook ground plan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pictorial_Handbook_of_London_(1854),_p._145_%E2%80%93_Plan_of_the_Temple_Church.jpg).
- [Inner Temple archive catalogue](https://www.innertemple.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Catalogue-for-Website.pdf):
  PLA/29 lists 37 Temple Church plans dated 1818–1960, including elevations and
  sections, for any future survey-grade refinement.

The exterior wall allowance (0.65 m), Round/chancel intersection, drum radius,
all exterior elevations, roof pitches, battlements, porch depth, buttresses,
window spacing and vault curves are visual approximations. Three parallel
chancel gables follow the historical exterior description in Godwin, p. 31.
Ancillary buildings, sculpture, tomb effigies and masonry ornament are omitted.
The transparent linework intentionally shows interior ribs through the shell.
